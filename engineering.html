Bio

Software Engineer at IDEX Biometrics

I have an MEng in Electronic and Electrical Engineering (2:1) from Imperial College London, and experience across circuit design, embedded systems and released mobile apps.

I have led the development and release of 8 iOS and Android apps, including one on the App Store, and the design of a hyperloop propulsion board in a multidisciplinary team. I also developed Python automation for robotic EMVCo standards testing, which is still in use. As Vice President Events, I managed an events team for a 1000+ member student society.

I combine strong project management with creative problem solving, through a self-led final year PCB project and an Arduino test system adopted across the engineering team, with 5+ systems in use.

Quick to adapt, I learnt React from scratch for a robotics project in a team of 12, and I am comfortable resolving a wide range of customer and partner issues. I am bilingual in English and Italian and speak Spanish (B1). Outside work, I have taken part in numerous theatre productions, in casts of over 50 people and venues seating over 450 people, building strong presentation, people and teamwork skills.
